# Bounce Back

A private, phone-friendly wellness companion: protein-first meals, water, rebounder workouts, supplements and personal goals.

**Open the app:** https://keithalanwitter-design.github.io/bounce-back/

## What it does

- **Today** – energy check-in, one-tap protein and water logging, a suggested workout, supplement checklist, a daily "little win", and goals written in your own words.
- **Kitchen** – 27 high-protein meal ideas, a "what's in my kitchen" matcher, a no-recipe plate builder, a weekly plan, and a grocery list that subtracts what you already have.
- **Move** – guided rebounder, stretch and breathing sessions with a hands-free interval timer (beeps and spoken cues), form cues and knee-protection rules.
- **Supplements** – track what you take and when, plus a plain-language guide to common supplements.
- **Progress** – seven-day views of protein, water, movement and energy, an optional weight trend, and a weekly reflection.
- **Reminders** – export to the phone's calendar for reliable daily alerts.

## Privacy

There are no accounts and no server. Everything entered is stored only in the browser on the device being used. Use Settings → "Save a backup file" to keep a copy.

## Install on a phone

- iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
- Android: open in Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

## Files

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; no build step. `data.js` holds the recipes, routines and supplement guide, so content can be edited without touching the app logic. When you change files, bump the version string in `sw.js`.

## Disclaimer

General wellness information, not medical advice. Talk with a doctor before changing diet, starting supplements, or beginning a new exercise routine.
